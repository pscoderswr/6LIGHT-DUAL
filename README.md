🎮 6LIGHT DUAL – PS5 DualSense 30 Lights to 6 Lights

6LIGHT DUAL is a Windows utility designed for supported PlayStation 5 DualSense controllers.

The application converts the controller's startup light behavior from approximately:

30 Lights → 6 Lights

and restores the short startup light behavior commonly seen on a new DualSense controller.

The entire conversion process is handled automatically by the 6LIGHT DUAL application.

No knowledge of the internal process is required.

---

✨ Features

- Convert approximately 30 blue flashes to 6 blue flashes
- Restore the 6-light startup state
- Recreate the startup light behavior commonly seen on a new DualSense controller
- Automatic controller detection
- Simple Windows interface
- Fast operation
- No complicated configuration
- Designed for supported PS5 DualSense controllers
- Standalone Windows application

---

🔵 What Is the 6-Light State?

A new PS5 DualSense controller commonly produces a short blue flashing sequence when the PS button is pressed.

This behavior is commonly referred to as the:

6-Light State

Previously used controllers may produce a much longer startup flashing sequence, often around:

30 Lights

6LIGHT DUAL is designed to convert this behavior from:

30 Lights → 6 Lights

and restore the short startup light sequence commonly associated with a new controller.

---

📦 Restore New-Controller Light Behavior

One of the recognizable behaviors of a fresh DualSense controller is its short startup flashing sequence.

6LIGHT DUAL is designed to restore this behavior on supported controllers.

After a successful operation, the controller can display approximately:

6 Blue Flashes

instead of the longer:

30 Blue Flashes

This restores the startup-light appearance commonly associated with a fresh or new DualSense controller.

6LIGHT DUAL was designed to make the process simple.

🔌 Controller Detection

Connect the DualSense controller to the computer using a USB cable that supports data transfer.

Launch 6LIGHT DUAL.

The application will automatically detect supported controllers.

If your controller is detected, you can start the conversion directly from the application.

---

▶️ How to Use

1. Connect the supported DualSense controller to the computer.
2. Launch 6LIGHT DUAL.
3. Wait for the controller to be detected.
4. Select the controller if required.
5. Press START.
6. Wait for the process to finish.
7. Test the controller.

After a successful conversion, the controller should produce approximately:

6 Blue Flashes

instead of approximately:

30 Blue Flashes
